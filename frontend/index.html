How often the index, next-snapshot times, and dashboard are refreshed in the background. Applies on the next cycle.

How often the open Dashboard view re-fetches the cached numbers (cheap; just a re-render when something changed).

How often per-snapshot reclaimable is recomputed in the background (heavy; keeps it always cached).

          
Excluded-folder tolerance
            

          
In the Excluded browser, a folder counts as "backed up" when less than this fraction of it is excluded (cuts "partial" noise).
